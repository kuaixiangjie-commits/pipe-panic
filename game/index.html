Pipe Panic! — Rainy night in Copperton, and every pipe in Harborview Apartments has burst at once. You are Pip Calloway, the plumber on call: WASD / arrows to move, hold E to repair a burst pipe or mop up the water, P to pause. Fix all six pipes before the 3:00 shift ends — if the water covers 75% of the floor, the building floods.
